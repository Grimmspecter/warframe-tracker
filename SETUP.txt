Warframe Mastery Checklist - website setup
==========================================

This puts the tracker on its own web address. Friends sign in with their
Google account; no Claude account needed. It's free and takes about 15 minutes.

Files in this folder
  index.html       the tracker
  config.js        your Firebase settings go here (step 4)
  firestore.rules  database rules to paste in (step 3)

------------------------------------------------------------
1. Create a Firebase project
------------------------------------------------------------
  - Go to https://console.firebase.google.com and sign in with Google.
  - Click "Create a project" (or "Add project").
  - Name it, for example: warframe-tracker
  - Google Analytics is optional. You can turn it off.
  - Click Create project, then Continue.

------------------------------------------------------------
2. Turn on Google sign-in
------------------------------------------------------------
  - In the left menu: Build > Authentication > Get started.
  - Sign-in method tab > Google > Enable.
  - Pick your email as the support email > Save.

------------------------------------------------------------
3. Create the database
------------------------------------------------------------
  - Left menu: Build > Firestore Database > Create database.
  - Pick a location near you > Next.
  - Choose "Start in production mode" > Create.
  - Open the Rules tab. Delete what's there, paste everything from
    firestore.rules, then click Publish.
    (These rules let every signed-in player read everyone's profile, and
    only change their own.)

------------------------------------------------------------
4. Get your settings into config.js
------------------------------------------------------------
  - Click the gear icon next to "Project Overview" > Project settings.
  - Under "Your apps", click the web icon  </>
  - Give it a nickname (anything) > Register app. Skip Firebase Hosting.
  - You'll see a block like:
        const firebaseConfig = {
          apiKey: "AIza...",
          authDomain: "warframe-tracker-xxxx.firebaseapp.com",
          ...
  - Open config.js in Notepad and replace each PASTE_HERE with the matching
    value. Keep the quotes. Save.

------------------------------------------------------------
5. Put the site online (GitHub Pages)
------------------------------------------------------------
  - Go to https://github.com and sign up or sign in.
  - Click "+" (top right) > New repository.
    Name: warframe-tracker   Public   > Create repository.
  - Click the "uploading an existing file" link.
  - Drag in index.html and your edited config.js > Commit changes.
  - Open the repository's Settings > Pages.
    Source: Deploy from a branch. Branch: main, folder: / (root) > Save.
  - After about a minute your site is at:
        https://YOUR-GITHUB-NAME.github.io/warframe-tracker/

------------------------------------------------------------
6. Allow sign-in from your site
------------------------------------------------------------
  - Back in Firebase: Build > Authentication > Settings tab >
    Authorized domains > Add domain.
  - Enter: YOUR-GITHUB-NAME.github.io  > Add.

------------------------------------------------------------
7. Use it
------------------------------------------------------------
  - Open your site, click "Sign in with Google", and set up your profile.
  - Send the address to your friends. They sign in with Google, set up a
    profile, and show up on the Friends tab and the Leaderboard.

Bring over your progress from the Claude version
  - On the Claude page: Overview > Backup and restore > Copy backup.
  - On your new site (signed in): Overview > paste it into the box > Restore.

Notes
  - The trader data (Baro Ki'Teer, Varzia, Darvo) loads by itself when the
    page opens. If it ever can't, paste the world state on the Traders tab.
  - Firebase's free plan is far more than a group of friends will use.
  - To update the site later, upload a new index.html to the same
    repository. Keep your config.js.
  - If sign-in says "unauthorized domain", recheck step 6.
